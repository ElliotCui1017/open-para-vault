# Knowledge Management

## Summary

A useful note system separates unprocessed source material from ideas that have been rewritten for reuse. The value comes from clarification, connection, and retrieval rather than collection volume.

## Key ideas

- Capture quickly, then clarify later.
- Give projects an outcome and next action.
- Promote reusable ideas into durable notes.

## Application

Use progressive summarization to keep the project guide concise while retaining its supporting rationale.
