# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific.

Project discussions should focus on improving the software, documentation, workflows, examples, and community experience.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disclosure of private information, and spam are not acceptable.

## Privacy

Because this project deals with personal knowledge workflows, do not ask contributors to share private notes, credentials, medical information, financial information, academic records, or other sensitive personal content to reproduce an issue. Prefer minimal fictional examples.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.
